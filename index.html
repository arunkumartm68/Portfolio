About

I'm an MCA graduate who likes building things you can walk around in. My main project, VRHome, lets you upload a 3D model of a house and tour it at real-world scale, in a desktop browser or inside a Meta Quest headset, with nothing to install.

From there I moved into Unreal Engine 5 and C++, placing life-size models in a real room through passthrough and letting you grab, turn and resize them with your bare hands. On the practical side, I built a face recognition attendance system with Flask, SQLite and dlib.

I use Arch Linux every day with a Hyprland desktop, and I'm comfortable fixing my own system from the terminal. I'm looking for entry-level roles and internships in software development, web development, and IT or systems work.

Projects

VRHome: landing page

Featured · Web app · WebXR

VRHome

Browser-based property tours in virtual reality. Upload a 3D model of a house and walk through it at real-world scale, on desktop or inside a Meta Quest 3 or 3S.

  • Loads GLB, GLTF, OBJ, FBX, STL, PLY, 3MF and DAE models.
  • An OBJ Web Worker merges meshes by material. One 30 MB export went from 116k+ meshes to a handful of draw calls.
  • Models over 20 MB are simplified to a 300k triangle budget before they are stored.
  • In VR you can repaint walls, place furniture, measure rooms, and grab the whole house with both hands.
  • Three.js
  • WebXR
  • Vite
  • IndexedDB
  • Web Workers

Mixed reality · Meta Quest 3S

Omnitrix Gallery

An alien museum in your room. Place glass display cases on the floor, on furniture or in mid-air, then fill them from a holographic collection.

  • Each alien is shown life size where the room allows, and the info panel says why when it is not.
  • Aliens wander, look at you, perform signature moves and react when you tap the glass.
  • Cases stay in place between sessions with spatial anchors.
  • Unreal Engine 5.7
  • C++

Web app · Computer vision

Face Recognition Attendance System

A webcam attendance system for classrooms. Students register their face once, and the scanner marks them present for the subject being taught.

  • dlib ResNet model with 128-D face descriptors, matched at a distance threshold of 0.45.
  • Separate dashboards for admin, staff and students, with Excel and PDF reports.
  • Accuracy is measured with leave-one-out and impostor tests. Nothing is hardcoded.
  • Python
  • Flask
  • SQLite
  • dlib
  • OpenCV
